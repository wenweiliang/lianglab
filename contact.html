Get in Touch

The Liang Lab is growing! We are building a team of curious, motivated people who enjoy asking questions, learning new approaches, and working together. As a new lab, we offer the opportunity to help shape our science and lab culture from the beginning.

Join Our Team

We value creativity, scientific rigor, open communication, and a supportive environment where everyone can grow as an independent thinker. You don't need to know everything when you join, but you should be excited to learn, take ownership of your work, and contribute to the team.

Postdoctoral Researchers

Are you ready to develop ambitious ideas and grow toward scientific independence? You'll have opportunities to lead projects, develop new skills, mentor junior scientists, and pursue publications and fellowships. We want to support your long-term career goals, wherever they take you.

How to apply

CV + two-page statement of research interests

Research Assistants

Do you enjoy hands-on problem-solving and being part of a collaborative team? You'll contribute to research, learn new techniques, and help establish a strong foundation for our growing lab. We value initiative, attention to detail, reliability, and clear communication. Prior wet-lab experience is preferred for experimental positions.

How to apply

CV + brief statement of research interests

Master's Students

Looking for a place to gain meaningful research experience? You'll work closely with lab members, build practical skills, and learn how to turn a scientific question into a thoughtful experiment or analysis. Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter most.

How to apply

CV + brief description of research interests

Ph.D. Students

We look forward to mentoring Ph.D. students at Baylor College of Medicine. Our goal is to help you develop confidence in asking questions, designing studies, interpreting results, and communicating your science. Rotation opportunities will be updated as arrangements are finalized.
