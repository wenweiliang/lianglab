Mapping the Uncharted Functional Landscape of Cancer

We integrate large-scale cancer multi-omics data with CRISPR-based functional genomics to uncover hidden biological mechanisms and therapeutic vulnerabilities across cancers.

The Questions

01 - Identify

What matters in cancer?

Identify functional regulators beyond protein-coding genes.

02 - Understand

How do these regulators work?

Uncover the molecular mechanisms that shape cancer cell behavior.

03 - Predict

Can we predict their effects?

Model regulatory dependencies, dosage sensitivity, and genetic interactions.
